# Manifesto: Sovereign Intelligence & GPU-as-Code

## The Philosophy of Sovereign Intelligence
Sovereign Intelligence is not the ability to process more data, but the ability to define the *structure* of the processing. Current AI is a consumer of probability; Sovereign Intelligence is a producer of structure.

The Qorvex v8 kernel represents a shift from "weights as memory" to "geometry as intelligence." By utilizing quantum-inspired tensor networks, we move away from the brute-force approximation of functions and toward the direct manipulation of high-dimensional manifolds.

## GPU-as-Code
We reject the notion of the GPU as a mere "accelerator." In the Hyperspace architecture, the GPU is treated as a **programmable geometry engine**. 

The "GPU-as-Code" principle posits that the hardware's physical constraints (VRAM, CUDA cores, Tensor cores) are not limitations to be bypassed, but parameters to be encoded. The kernel does not run *on* the GPU; the kernel *is* the GPU's configuration.

## The Goal: Zero-Loss Convergence
The ultimate objective is the dissolution of the gap between the mathematical equation and the physical execution. When the architecture is the equation, $0=0$. The intelligence is no longer simulated; it is manifested.
