Principles

Why we built this

01 / SOVEREIGN
Intelligence must not cross foreign borders
The moment your intelligence data touches a US company's infrastructure, it is accessible under FISA Section 702. For any non-US nation, this is not a theoretical risk — it is a structural compromise. GDI was built to eliminate this surface entirely.
02 / OPEN
Black boxes have no place in national security
Proprietary intelligence platforms are a trust problem disguised as a product. You cannot audit what you cannot read. GDI is deployed with full source access for authorised operators — every line auditable by the organisations that depend on it.
03 / RESILIENT
Designed for adversarial conditions
Internet connectivity is a privilege, not a guarantee. GDI was designed from the ground up to degrade gracefully: from full connectivity to partial to dark comms to fully air-gapped — capability at every level.
04 / CORRELATED
Data without correlation is just noise
Palantir sells dashboards. GDI sells understanding. The correlation engine — HEKTOR — finds the patterns human analysts miss because it processes all domains simultaneously, continuously, without fatigue.
